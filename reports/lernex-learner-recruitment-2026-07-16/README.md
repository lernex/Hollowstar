# Lernex learner recruitment campaign

Started: 2026-07-16 (America/Denver)

## Outcome target

The current founder-set target is 10 genuine external learners. This supersedes
the earlier five-tester checkpoint in the July 14-20 accountability plan.

The campaign is optimized for qualified learners who will use Lernex for real
study work and can be followed through D7 and W4. Reach, clicks, and form
submissions are diagnostic metrics, not the success metric.

## Cohort offer

- Up to 20 selected participants
- Seven-day product-research cohort
- At least 10 minutes of real studying per day
- Suitable for a class, exam, certification, or another concrete learning goal
- $10 after completing the cohort requirements
- Candid feedback is explicitly requested; praise is not expected
- Application: https://forms.gle/aExEefMf332hr4An6

## Funnel control

The public Google Form intentionally requires Google sign-in to reduce duplicate
or repeated submissions from the same person. This was confirmed by the founder
on 2026-07-17. Preserve the sign-in requirement unless the founder changes the
anti-duplicate design.

## Operating rules

1. Read each community's current rules immediately before posting.
2. Use one transparent founder identity; do not create sockpuppet accounts.
3. Do not paste the form into unrelated replies or send unsolicited mass DMs.
4. Pursue high volume through distinct, relevant placements where beta
   recruitment, product feedback, or promotion is allowed.
5. Tailor the first two lines to the audience and disclose the $10 completion
   incentive clearly.
6. Track the source on every applicant and follow each activated learner through
   D1, D7, and W4.
7. Stop a channel after removal, moderator objection, or two low-quality cycles.
8. On Product Hunt, keep company-focused posts inside Self-Promotion,
   Introduce Yourself, or an approved AMA. General-category Lernex/Metis essays
   were rejected on 2026-07-18, so use General primarily for useful replies to
   existing community questions rather than additional promotional threads.
